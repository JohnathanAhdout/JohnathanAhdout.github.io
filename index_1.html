Conditional Mutual Information for Screening Point-in-Time Macroeconomic Indicators Ongoing

Independent Research, Princeton University Department of Mathematics · 2026 – Present

Continuing project extending recent information-theoretic finance literature (Noguer i Alonso, 2025) from unconditional to conditional mutual information, in order to test whether a macroeconomic indicator remains informative about future returns once price history and other indicators are already known — a question ordinary correlation and linear regression cannot answer under nonlinear or regime-dependent relationships. Uses a k-nearest-neighbour (Frenzel–Pompe) conditional mutual information estimator with a leakage-safe local permutation significance test, applied to point-in-time indicators from the J.P. Morgan Macrosynergy Quantamental System (JPMaQS). Proposes pooling the estimator's neighbour search across the 20–50 currency areas JPMaQS covers per indicator to offset the estimator's known dimensionality limits, subject to an explicit poolability test, and a first-stage pruning step to keep full-universe conditional testing computationally tractable.

Black–Scholes Options Pricing & Risk Analysis Platform

Python · Jun 2026
